To be greeted with today's horoscope the first time you open Odoo each day:

1. Go to *My Profile > Preferences*.
2. Tick **Daily Horoscope Notification**.

The notification needs a birth date and a birth time on your employee profile:
natal positions depend on the time of day, so it is never guessed. A birth time
is local clock time, so its timezone must be resolvable too, either from the
birth coordinates or from the country of birth when that country was on a single
UTC offset that day. The coordinates themselves are not required here, as they
only place the Ascendant and the houses, which this reading does not use.

It summarises the transit closest to your natal chart. On a day where no transit
is close enough to be worth reading, nothing is shown. The full transit reading
stays available on the **Astral Chart** tab of your employee profile.

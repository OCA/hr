View your full Western astrological birth chart directly from your
employee profile.

Depends on ``hr_birth_data`` for the birth time and coordinates fields.

Adds an **Astral Chart** tab to the employee form showing:

-  **Sun Sign**, **Moon Sign** and **Ascendant** summary badges
-  An SVG zodiac wheel with planetary positions
-  A detailed table with degree, minute, sign and house for each planet
   (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune,
   Pluto, Chiron, Black Moon Lilith, Ceres)
-  Astrological houses (Whole Sign system) when birth time and location
   are provided
-  Current transits biwheel and interpretation
-  An optional daily horoscope notification, shown once a day when you
   open Odoo

Astronomical calculations use ``pyswisseph`` (Python binding for Swiss
Ephemeris) for high-precision planetary positions.

Configuration
=============

To be greeted with today's horoscope the first time you open Odoo each
day:

1. Go to *My Profile > Preferences*.
2. Tick **Daily Horoscope Notification**.

The notification needs both a birth date and a birth time on your
employee profile: natal positions depend on the time of day, so it is
never guessed. Birth coordinates are not required, as they only place
the Ascendant and the houses, which this reading does not use.

It summarises the transit closest to your natal chart. On a day where no
transit is close enough to be worth reading, nothing is shown. The full
transit reading stays available on the **Astral Chart** tab of your
employee profile.

To plan a working time for several employees at once (for example, a summer
schedule):

1.  Go to *Employees \> Employees* and switch to the list view.
2.  Select the employees.
3.  Click on *Actions \> Plan Working Time*.
4.  Fill the working time and the dates, and click on *Apply*.

The planning lines of each employee that overlap the period are cut, and the
line covering the whole period is split, so each employee recovers its own
working time after the period.
